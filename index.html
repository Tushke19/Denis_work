Important Disclaimer

Company is not responsible for offline meetings. If you meet someone from this platform in person, you do so entirely at your own risk. We strongly recommend:

  • Telling someone you know where you're going
  • Trusting your instincts
  • Not sharing personal identifying information until you're certain

What Company Is NOT

User Conduct

By entering Company, you agree to:
